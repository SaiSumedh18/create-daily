Notes on the fold

One sheet, three ideas.

  1. 01

    Folded in code

    Every facet is generated: diamond rings along the spine, a forked tail, pleated dorsal and paper pectorals. No models, no textures — colour is assigned face by face, like ink on a folded sheet.

  2. 02

    Swims like a fish

    A travelling sine wave runs head to tail, growing toward the fin. The faster it swims, the quicker the tail beats, and it banks gently into every turn.

  3. 03

    Follows you

    Move your pointer to lead it across the pond. Click or tap to drop a pellet — it will turn, chase and eat. Left alone, it wanders a slow lissajous loop.
